˚₊‧꒰ა Rules if You Lose Your Account ໒꒱‧₊˚

Account Loss & Recovery Guidelines

Lost access to your Monbooru account? Please read these guidelines before requesting assistance.

♡ 1. Account Ownership

  • You may only request recovery of an account that belongs to you.

  • You must provide sufficient information to establish that you are the original account owner.

  • Impersonating another user to claim their account is prohibited.

♡ 2. Lost Password

  • If you forget your password, use the available account recovery options, if provided.

  • Do not share your password with other users.

  • Monbooru staff will never ask you to disclose your password.

♡ 3. Lost Username

  • Try to locate your original username through your saved bookmarks or previous account references.

  • Do not claim an account simply because its username resembles yours.

♡ 4. Recovery Limitations

  • Monbooru does not support email-based account recovery.

  • Recovery assistance is not guaranteed.

  • If ownership cannot be reasonably verified, the account may be unrecoverable.

  • Do not expect staff to recover accounts without sufficient ownership information.

♡ 5. Account Security

  • Keep your login credentials somewhere secure.

  • Never share passwords or private authentication information.

  • Avoid using easily guessed passwords.

  • Be cautious of people offering unofficial account recovery services.

˚₊‧꒰ა PLEASE REMEMBER ໒꒱‧₊˚

Monbooru cannot guarantee the restoration of lost accounts. Keep your credentials safe and make sure you have a secure way to access them.

Prevention is better than losing access! ☆
